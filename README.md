## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates a Solar Lead Qualification & Booking Automation workflow built using **n8n**.

The goal is to help solar service providers manage customer inquiries, organize potential leads, and streamline the lead qualification and booking process through workflow automation.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Collect solar service inquiries through an online form.
- Organize submitted customer information.
- Process solar requirements and lead details.
- Demonstrate an automated lead management process.
- Improve the efficiency of customer inquiry handling.

## ⚙️ Tools & Technologies

- **n8n** – Workflow automation
- **Online Forms** – Customer information collection
- **Workflow Nodes** – Automated process management

## 🔄 Workflow Process

1. A customer submits a solar inquiry form.
2. The workflow receives the submitted information.
3. Customer and solar requirement details are processed.
4. The workflow handles the request according to its configured steps.
5. The resulting information is organized for further follow-up.
